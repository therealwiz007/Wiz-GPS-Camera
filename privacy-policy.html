Privacy Policy

Wiz GPS Camera

Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains how Wiz GPS Camera accesses and uses information when you use the app.

1. Information the app uses

  • Camera: Used to show the camera preview and take photographs.
  • Location: When you grant location permission, the app uses the device's location to show latitude, longitude, location accuracy and, where available, a readable address on the photo stamp.
  • Photographs: Images captured in the app are processed to add the selected location and date/time stamp.
  • Network access: Internet access may be used to retrieve map tiles or resolve an address. Map and geocoding services may receive technical request information, such as your IP address and the requested map area or coordinates, depending on the service used.

2. How information is used

Camera and location information is used to provide the app's core features: capturing photographs and adding a location stamp. Location is not required to take a photograph; if it is unavailable or permission is denied, location fields may be omitted or shown as unavailable.

3. Storage and sharing

The app is designed to save captured photographs on your device. The app does not require an account. It does not provide a Wiz Labs cloud photo-storage service. If you choose to share a photograph, the receiving app or service will handle it under its own privacy policy.

Photographs may contain visible location details and, where implemented, location metadata. Be mindful of this before sharing images publicly.

4. Location permissions and offline use

Location access is intended for use while you are using the camera. The app does not need background location access for its stated camera-stamping function. GPS coordinates can be obtained without an internet connection, but readable addresses and map imagery may be unavailable offline. Previously cached map imagery may be displayed when available.

5. Third-party services

The app may use third-party map tile or geocoding services to display maps or resolve addresses. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. The specific providers and their policies should be identified here before the app is publicly released.

6. Data security and retention

Photos saved by the app remain in device storage unless you choose to share or move them. You can manage or delete those photos using the app or your device's gallery/file-management tools. No online account is needed to use the app.

7. Children's privacy

Wiz GPS Camera is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account system.

8. Your choices

  • You can deny camera or location permissions in Android settings. Camera permission is needed to take photos; location permission is optional.
  • You can turn off location stamping by denying location permission or, when available, disabling location fields in stamp settings.
  • You control whether and where you share captured photographs.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or service providers change. The effective date above will be revised when a new version is published.
