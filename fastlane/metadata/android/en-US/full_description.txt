Wiz GPS Camera stamps exact location, address, map and time on your photos,
so every picture is proof of where and when it was taken.

PROOF YOU CAN TRUST
• Fresh, precise GPS: the camera opens only after a fresh fix (accuracy shown)
• Network time check warns you if the phone clock is wrong
• Mock-location detection: flag the photo or block capture
• Tamper evidence: each saved photo is fingerprinted (SHA-256) and the gallery
  marks edited files (verifiable in this app on the same phone)

YOUR STAMP, YOUR WAY
• Styles: Glass, Classic, Light, Bold, Minimal; templates with map, compact or text
• Address, coordinates (decimal, DMS, UTM, MGRS), altitude, compass, speed,
  plus code, weather (optional), notes, project name, your logo
• 21 time zones, 12/24-hour clock, metric or imperial

PRO CAMERA
• Up to 16 MP, best-quality capture, flash and torch, exposure, zoom presets,
  grid, level, timer, burst, volume-key shutter

FIELD WORK
• Projects and quick tags (Before, After, Damage…)
• Gallery with search, filters and a map of your photos
• Export PDF reports, CSV or KML (Google Earth)

Photos stay on your phone and no account is needed. The internet is used for
map tiles, address lookup, network time, optional weather and Play updates.
