Wiz GPS Camera is a GPS map camera that stamps every photo with where and when it was taken: the street address, a live map, latitude and longitude, and the date and time. Use it as proof of a site visit, an inspection, a delivery or work done.

LOCATION STAMP ON EVERY PHOTO
• Full address and place name, added automatically
• Small live map with a pin, printed right on the photo
• Latitude and longitude in decimal, DMS, UTM or MGRS
• Date and time in 12 or 24 hour format, with 21 time zones
• Optional extras: altitude, GPS accuracy, speed, compass heading, Plus Code, weather and your own logo
• Add a note or quick tags such as Before, After or Damage

PHOTOS YOU CAN TRUST
• The camera opens only after a fresh, precise GPS fix
• Mock (fake) GPS detection: flag it on the photo, or block the shot
• Time is checked against network time, so a wrong phone clock can't change it
• Each photo gets a unique ID, and the gallery shows whether a photo was edited after it was taken
• GPS coordinates are also written to the photo's EXIF data

PROJECTS AND REPORTS FOR FIELD WORK
• File photos by project: each project gets its own folder, and its name goes on the stamp
• Search photos by note, address or ID, and filter by project
• See every photo on a map
• Export a PDF report, CSV spreadsheet or KML file for Google Earth, then share it

STAMP STYLES
• Layouts: full with map, compact, or text only
• Styles: Glass, Classic, Light, Bold and Minimal
• Choose the position, accent colour, card opacity and text size
• A live preview on the camera shows exactly how the photo will look

A CAMERA THAT FEELS FAMILIAR
• Big shutter in the middle, gallery on one side and lens switch on the other
• Flash (off, auto, on, torch), self-timer, burst, grid, level and exposure
• Pinch or tap to zoom, tap to focus
• Volume buttons take a photo
• Smooth animations that stay light on battery and memory, even on low-end phones

WHO IT'S FOR
Site engineers, surveyors, construction and real-estate teams, insurance and loss assessors, delivery and field-service staff, petrol pump and dealer audits, sales visits, travellers and anyone who needs a photo with date, time and location.

PRIVACY
Photos stay on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up and no ads. Location is used only to make the stamp. The internet is used only for map tiles, the address lookup, network time and optional weather.
